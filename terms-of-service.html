In short: AI Clipping Pipeline is an automation tool, operated by a single person, that turns long-form video into short clips and posts them to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube accounts the operator has explicitly connected. 01

What this service does

AI Clipping Pipeline is an automated content pipeline: it analyzes source video, selects and edits short-form clips, and publishes them to social platforms on behalf of accounts that have been connected to it. It is operated as a single-person tool, not a public multi-tenant platform — every account it publishes to belongs to the operator or to a creator who has directly authorized it.

02

Connecting an account

Connecting a TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube account happens through that platform's own official OAuth sign-in — this service never sees or stores a platform password. Authorizing access grants the pipeline permission to publish video content to that account under the scopes shown on the platform's own consent screen at the time of authorization.

Until a platform's own app review is complete, some platforms (TikTok in particular) restrict posting to the connected account itself rather than the public.

03

Acceptable use

  • Only connect accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to publish on behalf of.
  • Only submit source video you have the rights to clip and redistribute.
  • Do not use the pipeline to publish content that violates the destination platform's own community guidelines — those guidelines still apply in full to anything this tool posts.
04

Content ownership

The operator claims no ownership over source video or the clips generated from it. Ownership and rights remain exactly as they were before the pipeline touched the content; the pipeline acts purely as an editing and publishing tool.

05

No warranty

The pipeline is provided as-is. Automated clip selection and publishing can make mistakes — a wrong cut, a missed post, a platform-side rejection. Review is expected before anything published matters commercially or reputationally.

06

Revoking access

Any connected account owner can revoke this pipeline's access at any time, directly from that platform's own app-permissions settings (e.g. TikTok's or Instagram's connected-apps page). Revocation takes effect immediately on the platform's side; the pipeline stops being able to publish to that account as soon as the platform invalidates the token.
