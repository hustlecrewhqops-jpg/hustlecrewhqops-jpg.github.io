AI Clipping Pipeline

A single-operator automation tool that turns long-form video into short clips and publishes them to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube accounts the operator has explicitly connected.

01

What it does

Selects, edits, and captions short-form video clips, then publishes them to social accounts that have been connected through each platform's own official OAuth sign-in.

02

Who it's for

Operated as a single-person tool, not a public multi-tenant platform — every account it publishes to belongs to the operator or to a creator who has directly authorized it.

03

Your control

Any connected account owner can revoke access at any time from that platform's own app-permissions settings. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full detail.
